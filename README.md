### 🧠 About me

I'm a CS grad student who lives at the intersection of **offensive security and systems engineering**. I like taking things apart to understand how they break — and then building the defenses. My day-to-day runs from **penetration testing and web app security** to **blockchain development**, **backend services**, and **server/network configuration**.

- 🔭 Currently working on **embedded security** — Trusted Firmware-M (TF-M) on Zephyr.
- 🛡️ Building offensive tooling, most recently the [Redis Pentest Toolkit](https://github.com/faintdono/Redis-Pentest-Toolkit).
- 🌱 Deepening my knowledge of cryptography, cloud security, and secure firmware.
- 💬 Ask me about pentesting, blockchain, or backend architecture.
- 📫 Reach me by **email** or **LinkedIn** — open to collaboration.
